I'm a PhD (DPhil) candidate in Finance at the University of Oxford’s Saïd Business School and a member of the Oxford-Man Institute of Quantitative Finance. My work sits at the intersection of macro-finance and asset pricing, with interests in financial econometrics, monetary economics, and fiscal–monetary policy interactions. I previously completed the MPhil in Economics at Oxford and undergraduate studies in Mathematics, Computer Science, and Statistics at the University of Sydney.

I have previous experience at SPX Capital (Macro hedge fund), Goldman Sachs, Amazon, and Insurance Australia Group.
